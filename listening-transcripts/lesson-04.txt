### Dialogue 4

Johnson began working as a tour guide at age 22.
He worked in another country once a month.
He was happy because he could visit a different country and work at the same time.
He enjoyed his life.
Now he is 24 and wants to start a new job as a travel reporter.

**Question:** What do we know about Johnson?

### Dialogue 5

Sometimes they go swimming with people in their hotel.
Many people like their hotel.
Tina's friend, Sandy, goes there twice a month.
