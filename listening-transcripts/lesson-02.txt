## 一、辨識句意（Identify the meaning）

1. The weather was nice, so they walked the dog in the park.

2. Jesse had a bad day yesterday because she got 50 points on the test.

3. Karen visited her grandparents two days ago.

4. Tim and his dad washed the car this morning.

5. John was scared last night because he watched a ghost movie on TV.

## 三、言談理解（Dialogue comprehension）

### Dialogue 1

Did you go to the basketball game with Susan yesterday?
I'd have liked to, but I needed to study for the test.

**Question:** Did the boy go to the basketball game yesterday?

### Dialogue 2

I can't go to the movies with you and Jenny this afternoon.
Why not?
My mom just called me.
She has to work, so I need to cook dinner for my sisters.

**Question:** Which is true about the girl's mom?

### Dialogue 3

Mia, why is Tim crying?
Did his brother hit him?
No, his cat ran away last night and he couldn't find it.
Oh, that's too bad.

**Question:** Why is Tim crying?

### Dialogue 4

Dad bought a beautiful dress for mom today.
Why?
Today was mom's birthday, and I made a cake for mom.
Oh no, I didn't buy anything for her.

**Question:** What did their dad do today?

### Dialogue 5

Emma had English class at 8.30 this morning.
Then, she had math class at 9.25.
Before she had her favorite class, she had art class, But she doesn't like art class because she is not good at drawing.

**Question:** What time did Emma have math class?
