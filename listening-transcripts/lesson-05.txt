## 一、辨識句意（Identify the meaning）

1. There are two convenience stores near my house.

2. It only takes a few minutes to buy an ice black tea.

3. People are eating in the dining area.

4. The girl paid 8 dollars and 40 cents for the two bags of chocolates.

5. I have to go to the restroom.

## 二、基本問答（Basic Q&A）

1. How's your trip so far?

2. You can do many things in this area.

3. My new phone case cost me about $2,000 NT dollars.

4. Can you also get your train ticket there?

5. How long does it take to get to your school by bus?
