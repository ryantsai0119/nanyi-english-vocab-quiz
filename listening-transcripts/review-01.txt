## 一、辨識句意（Identify the meaning）

1. Last weekend, Mom and I cleaned the kitchen.

2. They chatted with each other on the bus after school.

3. My family visited our grandparents this summer.

4. John didn't go to school because he was sick.

5. Nancy didn't buy the phone because she didn't have enough money.

## 二、基本問答（Basic Q&A）

1. Why were you so angry this morning?

2. What did you do this weekend?

3. What did you have for breakfast?

4. Why weren't you at home yesterday?

5. Maybe we can visit the art museum this week.

## 三、言談理解（Dialogue comprehension）

### Dialogue 1

One.
My family went to Alishan three days ago.
How was the trip?
Great!
I love the tall trees and beautiful lake.
How was the weather?
It was cool.
April is a great time to visit there.

**Question:** When did the girl go to Alishan?

### Dialogue 2

Did you play basketball with Jack yesterday morning?
No.
I was at Uncle Bill's house.
He helped me with my math homework.

**Question:** What did the boy do yesterday morning?

### Dialogue 3

What did you do last Sunday?
I went to Taidong with my classmates.
Did you buy anything when you were there?
Sure.
I bought a special pair of shoes.
Take a look.
Here they are.
Cool.
They are special.

**Question:** What did the boy buy in Taidong?

### Dialogue 4

Good afternoon.
May I help you?
Yes.
Mother's Day is coming.
I need to buy a gift for my mom.
How about a bag?
Wow!
It's beautiful!
That's it!

**Question:** Why did the girl need to buy a gift?
