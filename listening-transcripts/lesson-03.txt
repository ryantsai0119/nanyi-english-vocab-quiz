## 一、辨識句意（Identify the meaning）

1. There is a hairy spider on the table.

2. Some students were climbing a tree when we passed by.

3. Before the boy went out, his father came back.

4. After she drank a cup of hot tea, she ate some bread.

5. When the woman arrived at the party, everyone was surprised.

## 二、基本問答（Basic Q&A）

1. What were you doing at seven last night?

2. When I took out my bread, a monkey suddenly came and took it away?

3. Why was Lisa all wet when she came home?

4. What was Lisa doing while Tom was cooking in the kitchen?

5. Helen was tired, but she was still trying.
