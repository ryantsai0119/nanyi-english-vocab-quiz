1. My uncle was an English teacher ten years ago, but now he is a farmer.

3. I watched a ghost movie last night.

4. Tina washed her dog yesterday morning.

5. My sister studies hard every weekend.

2. Where did your brother go yesterday?

## 三、言談理解（Dialogue comprehension）

### Dialogue 1

Mark, did you walk the dog today?
No, I didn't.
But Miley did.
She also cooked dinner.
Great.
Then, what did you do today?
I cleaned the kitchen with Hank.

**Question:** Who cooked dinner?

### Dialogue 2

John, I didn't see you at the party last night.
I was taking care of my sister at home.
She didn't feel right.
I'm sorry.
How is she?
She's fine.
Thank you.

**Question:** How is John's sister?

### Dialogue 3

Mom, can I take a day off today?
Why?
Billy, are you okay?
Yes, Mom.
But I didn't study for my math test today.
What did you do last night?
I did my English and math homework.
Is that all?
Well, I played a few video games.
Billy, you can't take a day off.
You must go to school.
Mom, can I?
The bus is coming.

**Question:** Did Billy take a day off?

### Dialogue 4

Hi, Joe.
This is Wendy.
Hello, Wendy.
How about swimming today?
Today is Friday.
Maybe not.
I did it last Saturday morning.
Then how about basketball?
No.
I did that last Saturday afternoon.
Are you at home all day?
Yes.
I'm going to study for the test.
Okay.
Good luck.

**Question:** What sports did Joe do last weekend?

### Dialogue 5
