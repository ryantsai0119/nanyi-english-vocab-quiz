## 一、辨識句意（Identify the meaning）

1. I had dinner with my family today.

2. Today is father's day, and my birthday is tomorrow.

3. Jack told an interesting story at the party.

4. Jimmy is my uncle, and he is also my English teacher.

5. Jane plays the guitar very well.

## 二、基本問答（Basic Q&A）

1. What are you going to do later?

2. Did you enjoy yourselves tonight?

3. Will you go to the movies with us tomorrow?

4. What are you going to do this weekend?

5. Who will go to the potluck with you?

## 三、言談理解（Dialogue comprehension）

### Dialogue 1

Today is your birthday.
You can choose your gift in this shop and it's on me.
Really?
Wow, thank you.
I want this pink dress and that pink bag.
No problem.
Wait.
Oh no.
I can't find my bag.
Maybe you forgot to bring it.
Don't worry.
I have some money.

**Question:** Who spent the money on the birthday gifts?

### Dialogue 2

Hello.
May I help you?
Sure.
I'm looking for an apartment with three rooms.
What else do you want for your dream apartment?
I have two cats and I like cooking.
Well, we have one at a low price.
You'll love the kitchen.

**Question:** What do we know about the man and the woman?

### Dialogue 3

Where's Eric?
He went to the movies.
Did he go with his brother?
No, he went by himself.

**Question:** What do we know about Eric?

### Dialogue 4

Why did you end up with so many things?
Didn't you just want to buy a pair of shoes?
I did, but there was a big sale today.
Buy two pairs and get one shopping bag for free.
But here are two shopping bags.
I think you'll need one too.

**Question:** How many shoes did the woman buy?
